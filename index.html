Our Policies

Shipping

Delivery within Alexandria takes 2-4 working days. All orders are hand-delivered locally by our team to ensure premium handling and condition upon arrival.

Returns & Exchanges

Returns and exchanges are accepted within 14 days of receiving your order, provided the item is unworn, unwashed, and in its original packaging.

Care Instructions

To protect the 380GSM fabric and DTF prints: Machine wash cold, inside out. Do not iron directly on the print. Hang dry naturally to maintain the oversized structure.

Custom Orders

Custom printed items are non-refundable unless there is a manufacturing defect. We ensure the highest quality DTF printing, but please ensure your uploaded artwork is clear.
